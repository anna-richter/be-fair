**Task Type:** Image classification (binary classification)

**Input Format:**  
- Input images are RGB JPEG files located in /sc-scratch/sc-scratch-ikim-guidlight/be-fair/mlzero/addition_1_data/MyImages/, referenced by image_name in the CSV files.

**Prediction Output Format:**  
- For each input image, output a malignancy probability (a float between 0 and 1) indicating the likelihood that the lesion is malignant.

**Additional Details:**  
- The model is trained to classify skin lesion images as malignant or benign.
- The evaluation metric is Area Under the Receiver Operator Curve (AUROC).
- The model should be fair towards skin tone.
